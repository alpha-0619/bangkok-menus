Baan Suriyasai (Surawong) บ้านสุริยาศัย (สาขาบางรัก)

Bang Rak, Bangkok

Menu published by the restaurant. Seen 2026-10-20. Date printed on menu: 2025 (file name of the a la carte PDF). Open the original menu.

The menu prints: “All prices are subject to 10% service charge and 7% government tax.”. So the bill is more than the menu price: 10% service charge is added, then 7% VAT on the price plus the service charge (Thai VAT is charged on the full amount, service fees included). A 1,000 THB dish comes to about 1,177 THB. “bill ≈” under each price is that amount, rounded.
